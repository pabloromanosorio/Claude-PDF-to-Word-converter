# Simple User Guide: PDF to Word Converter

Welcome! This tool helps you convert PDF files into editable Word documents (DOCX) using advanced AI. It preserves formatting, tables, and text better than standard converters.

## 🚀 How to Start the App

### For Mac Users
**What opens this?** The built-in **Terminal** app (it's on every Mac).

1.  Look for the file named **`start_app.command`**.
2.  **Double-click** it.
3.  **If it opens:** Great! A black window will appear. Wait for the browser.
4.  **If it says "Unidentified Developer" or "Cannot be opened":**
    *   **Don't panic!** This is normal for apps not from the App Store.
    *   **Right-Click** (or Control-Click) the file.
    *   Select **Open** from the menu.
    *   Click **Open** in the pop-up box.
5.  **If it says "You do not have appropriate access privileges":**
    *   Open the "Terminal" app (Command+Space, type "Terminal").
    *   Type `chmod +x ` (with a space at the end).
    *   Drag and drop the `start_app.command` file into the Terminal window.
    *   Press **Enter**.
    *   Now try double-clicking the file again.

### For Windows Users
**What opens this?** The built-in **Command Prompt** (it's on every PC).

1.  Look for the file named **`install_and_run.bat`**.
2.  **Double-click** it.
3.  A black window will appear. **Do not close this window.**
4.  Your web browser will open automatically.
5.  **If Windows says "Windows protected your PC":**
    *   Click **"More info"**.
    *   Click **"Run anyway"**.

## ⚙️ First Time Setup (One Time Only)

To use the AI, you need an "API Key" from Anthropic (the makers of Claude).

1.  **Get your Key**: Go to [https://console.anthropic.com/](https://console.anthropic.com/) and sign up.
2.  **Create Key**: Look for "API Keys" and click "Create Key".
3.  **Copy Key**: Copy the long text that starts with `sk-ant-...`.
4.  **Enter in App**:
    *   In the PDF Converter app (in your browser), click the **Settings** icon (⚙️) or look for the "API Key" button.
    *   Paste your key into the box.
    *   Click **Save**.

You only need to do this once! The app will remember your key.

## 📝 How to Convert a PDF

1.  **Select File**: Click the big **"Select PDF"** area (or drag and drop your file there).
2.  **Choose Model** (Optional):
    *   **Haiku**: Faster and cheaper. Good for simple documents.
    *   **Sonnet**: Smarter and better quality. Best for complex layouts, handwriting, or tables.
3.  **Convert**: Click the **"Convert to Word"** button.
4.  **Wait**: You will see a progress bar. The AI is reading and rewriting your document.
    *   *Note: Large files may take 1-2 minutes.*
5.  **Download**: Once finished, a **"Download DOCX"** button will appear. Click it to save your Word file!

## ❓ Troubleshooting

### The browser didn't open?
If the black window is running but no website appeared:
1.  Open your browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox).
2.  Type `http://localhost:3000` in the address bar and press Enter.

### "Invalid API Key" error?
*   Check that you copied the entire key (it should start with `sk-ant-`).
*   Make sure you have "credits" in your Anthropic account. New accounts usually get some free credits, but you may need to add a payment method eventually.

### Conversion failed?
*   **Try a different model**: If you used Haiku, try Sonnet.
*   **Check the black window**: It often shows error messages that can help explain what went wrong.
*   **File too big?**: Extremely large PDFs (over 50MB) might be too much. Try splitting the PDF if possible.

### How to close the app?
1.  Close the browser tab.
2.  Go to the black Terminal window and press any key, or just close the window.
